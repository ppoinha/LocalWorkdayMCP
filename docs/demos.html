Demos

See it answer real questions

Four short recordings of plain-English questions answered from Workday data, with charts drawn from the answers. Recorded on a Workday demo tenant. Aggregates only, no individual records.

Headcount and FTE

Headcount and FTE by function, and how headcount has changed over the years.

Questions asked

  • “Show headcount and FTE by function”
  • “How has headcount changed over the years?”

Diversity

Women’s share of each function, and the workforce by generation.

Questions asked

  • “What share of each function is women?”
  • “What does our workforce look like by generation?”

Pay comparison

Average base pay by grade and gender, and which compensation scorecards exist.

Questions asked

  • “What is average base pay by grade and gender?”
  • “Which compensation scorecards exist?”

Recruiting

Open job postings and where they are located.

Questions asked

  • “Show open job postings”
  • “Where are the open postings located?”
Privacy and safety. Use Workday MCP with Claude Enterprise, which does not use your conversations to train models. Whatever Claude retrieves is sent to the AI provider as part of the conversation.
