No intermediary between you and your data

User security stays in Workday

Two layers, both controlled by you

1

The API Client sets the outer limit

Your Workday administrator chooses its scopes when they register the client. Nobody can reach beyond them.

2

The user's own permissions decide the rest

People sign in with their own Workday account and approve the client. Workday applies their security groups to every call.

What you keep

  • Your existing Workday permissions
  • No new passwords or user directory
  • API activity visible in Workday
  • Switch access off in Workday at any time

Protected by design

Read-only, with sensitive data held back

Read-only

No write tools

DaySight MCP has no tools that change data, and the Workday client refuses anything that is not a read request.

Always removed

Personal details

Home and personal contact details, national IDs and bank details are removed from every answer.

Never returned

Most sensitive

Religion and sexual orientation are never read. Names and contact details of job candidates are never returned.

Small groups

Hidden under 3

Group results under a minimum size are suppressed. The size is adjustable in the settings.

Worker names, work email and work phone are returned when you ask for them, if the signed-in person's Workday permissions allow it.

Stays on your computer

Secrets never reach the AI

  • No client secret is needed with PKCE. If you use one, it stays on your PC.
  • Your login token is stored encrypted in your Windows user profile.
  • Secrets and tokens never go into tool arguments, results or logs, so the model never sees them.
  • There is no generic "call any URL" tool. Your assistant can only use the Workday operations the server exposes.
  • Audit logs record the tool name, argument names, status and duration, never values.
  • Every release has a SHA-256 checksum beside the download, so you can verify the file.

The one decision for leadership

Claude Enterprise or ChatGPT Enterprise for privacy and safety

Whatever your assistant retrieves is sent to the AI provider as part of the conversation. For HR data, use Claude Enterprise or ChatGPT Enterprise with data sharing and model training turned off. Both providers say that business data is not used for training by default, so check these settings in your own plan, and check your company's policy on sending HR data to an AI service.

A checklist before you start

  • Have your HRIS administrator register the API Client, and never share a client secret.
  • Give it the smallest set of scopes that covers your questions.
  • Start on a sandbox or implementation tenant.
  • Use Claude Enterprise or ChatGPT Enterprise rather than a consumer account, with data sharing and model training turned off.
