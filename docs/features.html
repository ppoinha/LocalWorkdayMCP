Features

Built to be easy to start and safe to leave on

Workday MCP is a Model Context Protocol server for the Workday REST API, packaged as a Claude Desktop extension.

Ask about

Eight areas of Workday. Choose which ones Claude may use with the "Workday areas" setting.

W

Workers

Who someone is, their job history, business title changes, direct reports, inbox tasks and pay slips.

O

Organizations

Supervisory organizations, who manages them, who is in them, and how teams fit together.

S

Staffing

Jobs and positions, staffing information and job change reasons.

A

Absence and time off

Your balances, your plans, scheduled entries, and which absence types you can request.

C

Compensation

Compensation scorecards, plans and related reference data you are permitted to see.

P

Performance and goals

Your goals, development plans and mentorships.

R

Recruiting

Open job postings and requisitions, with their dates and locations.

T

Talent

Talent pipeline and development reference data where your security group allows it.

Areas beyond workers and organizations arrive in version 0.2.0 and later and each needs its scope on your API Client. Workday decides what you can see, so some questions return less for people without the matching permissions.

New in 0.6.0

Workforce analytics

Optional and off by default. Switch on Allow Workday Query Language (WQL) reports to ask about the whole organization, not just your own team.

1

Executive scorecard

One page of 25 figures covering size, movement, diversity, cost and structure, each against the previous period, with places that stand out.

2

Slice it any way

25 dimensions such as function, region, location, cost center, grade and tenure, 34 measures, and 29 ready-made views for the board, CEO, CHRO, CFO and HR business partners.

3

Narrow to one place

Add a country, location, function or leader to any view for a local team snapshot or a business unit review.

4

Workforce report as a PDF

Ask for a multi-page workforce report. Sections without enough data are left out and listed with the reason.

5

Private by default

Aggregates only. No names, no individual pay, groups smaller than three people are hidden and pay needs groups of five or more.

6

Built for big organizations

The worker data is read once and kept in memory for a few minutes, so follow-up questions are quick and do not hit Workday again.

New and still being verified on real tenants. Check the first figures against a Workday report you trust. See example questions.

Easy to set up, easy to use

1

One file, no developer tools

Claude Desktop runs the extension with its own built-in Node.js. You do not install Node.js, Git or npm.

2

A short form

Client ID, client secret, two endpoints and a redirect address. The form has sensible defaults for the rest.

3

Sign in once

The login is stored encrypted in your Windows profile and refreshed automatically, so you normally sign in once.

4

Just talk

No query language, no report builder. Ask in plain English and follow up the way you would in any conversation.

Safe by design

✓

Your access, not more

Two layers apply on every request: the scopes of your own API Client set the outer limit and your Workday permissions decide what comes back.

✓

Secrets stay local

The client secret and tokens stay on your computer and never reach the model.

✓

No "call any URL" tool

Claude can only use the specific Workday operations the server exposes.

✓

Changes are off by default

Every tool that creates, updates or deletes data is hidden until you switch on "Allow changes in Workday".
