Claude Desktop extension for Workday

Ask Claude about your Workday data in plain English.

Workday MCP connects Claude to Workday. Ask analytical questions about your workforce and get answers, charts and dashboards back. You sign in as yourself, so Claude sees what you are allowed to see, and nothing more.

How to installSee example questions

Early testing. Runs on your own Windows PC through Claude Desktop. Free for non-commercial use. For privacy and safety, use it with Claude Enterprise, which does not use your conversations to train models.

How it works

From download to first answer in four steps

  1. Register your API Client

    In Workday, you create your own API Client and choose its scopes. Your client ID and secret are yours alone.

  2. Install the extension

    In Claude Desktop, open Settings, Extensions, Advanced settings and pick the downloaded file.

  3. Fill in the short form

    Paste your client details and endpoints, and switch on the analytics option.

  4. Sign in and ask

    Ask Claude to call workday_login, approve in your browser, then start asking questions.
