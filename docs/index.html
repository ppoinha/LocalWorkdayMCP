Workforce analytics for Workday

Ask about your Workday data in plain English.

DaySight MCP connects your AI assistant to Workday. Ask analytical questions about your workforce and get answers, charts and dashboards back. You sign in as yourself, so your assistant sees what you are allowed to see, and nothing more.

How to installWatch the demos

Recorded on a Workday demo tenant. Aggregates only, no individual records. Early testing, runs on your own Windows PC through Claude Desktop, with a free 30-day trial for each Workday tenant. For privacy and safety, use it with Claude Enterprise, which does not use your conversations to train models.

How it works

From download to first answer in four steps

  1. Register your API Client

    In Workday, you create your own API Client and choose its scopes. Your client is yours alone, and with PKCE it needs no secret.

  2. Install the extension

    In Claude Desktop, open Settings, Extensions, Advanced settings and pick the downloaded file.

  3. Fill in the short form

    Paste your client details and endpoints.

  4. Sign in and ask

    Ask your assistant to call workday_login, approve in your browser, then start asking questions.
